# CIA Triad

[← Back to index](../README.md)

The CIA Triad is the core model of information security.

| Goal | Meaning | Controls | Example of violation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Confidentiality** | Only authorized people can access information | Encryption, access control, MFA | Data breach, leaked passwords |
| **Integrity** | Data stays accurate and unaltered | Hashing, digital signatures, version control | Tampered records, malicious UPDATE query |
| **Availability** | Systems and data are accessible when needed | Backups, redundancy, DDoS protection | Ransomware, DDoS attack |

## Key points
- When analyzing any incident, ask which of C, I, and A was affected.
- The three goals can conflict: stricter security may reduce availability or usability.
- Related terms: authentication, authorization, accountability, non-repudiation.

## My Reflection
I learned that the CIA Triad is an important foundation of cybersecurity. Confidentiality protects information from unauthorized access, Integrity keeps data accurate, and Availability ensures that systems and information can be accessed when needed. This lesson helped me understand why protecting data is not only about preventing hackers but also about maintaining the reliability of systems. I can apply these principles when developing applications and managing information.
